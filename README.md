# Financial Crime Investigation & Blockchain Forensics

Shalem Raj Zacharaiah — Cybersecurity MSc graduate, University of Surrey.

Independent investigation projects covering AML typologies, blockchain
transaction tracing, and fraud analysis, built to demonstrate practical
financial crime investigation skills.

This is an independent reconstruction of a publicly documented incident, using primary sources and on-chain data to trace the fund flow and verify the established attribution. The goal is to demonstrate investigative methodology, not to present original findings.

## Projects
Tracing $620M stolen by the Lazarus Group from the Ronin bridge in March 2022. Traces the full fund flow from the bridge withdrawal through stablecoin conversion, dispersal across 22 addresses, and into Tornado Cash, reconciling 182,163 ETH against the amount stolen. Primary-source attribution, on-chain analysis in SQL, and a documented account of where the trail ends and why.
