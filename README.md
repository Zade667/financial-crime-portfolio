## Projects

**[Ronin Bridge Hack — On-Chain Investigation](https://github.com/Zade667/Ronin_bridge_investigation)**

Tracing $620M stolen by the Lazarus Group from the Ronin bridge in March 2022.
Follows the full fund flow from the bridge withdrawal through stablecoin
conversion, dispersal across 22 addresses, and into Tornado Cash, reconciling
182,163 ETH against the amount stolen. Primary-source attribution, on-chain
analysis in SQL, and a documented account of where the trail ends and why.
